## About Meera
- Spent two years in pharmaceutical formulation before starting Skinstinct.
- Not a dermatologist, no medical degree.
- In 2021, in a stability review at the pharma company, a cosmeceutical at
  pH 3.2 carried a 24-month stability claim; the active degrades below
  pH 3.8. Nobody else asked. It passed review. She left 7 months later.

## About Skinstinct products and practice
- Serum pH is 5.5-5.8, documented on every batch.
- Contract manufacturer brief requires pH documentation, stability testing at
  three-month intervals for the first year, and mid-batch CoA sampling.
- Spent several months adjusting the serum base before CoA results matched
  application consistency tests; the label percentage never changed.
- Everything is fragrance-free, including preservative system, emollients and
  carrier ingredients.
- Does not use parabens (partly pragmatic, partly customer trust), but does not
  believe they are dangerous at cosmetic concentrations.
- Ceramide approach: ceramide NP and AP, paired with cholesterol and fatty
  acids. No product is marketed primarily on barrier-repair claims.
- Publishes what product testing covers on the website: sample size, study
  type, who ran it. Some testing is early stage.
- Does NOT currently sell: a Vitamin C product (14+ months in development), a
  peptide product, a sunscreen.
- Two products in formulation, not ready to discuss in detail.

## Numbers already published
- 12 months to June 2025: 23% of returns cited texture/feel; 71% of those came
  from cities with average annual humidity above 70% (Mumbai, Chennai, Kochi,
  Kolkata). After reformulating to a lighter base, humid-city returns dropped
  to 8% the following quarter. Active concentrations unchanged.
- Repeat purchase rate at 12 months: 67% (DTC skincare typically 20-40%).
- Humid-city return rate was almost three times the rate elsewhere before
  the reformulation.
- Has explained the no-fragrance decision "a few hundred times".
